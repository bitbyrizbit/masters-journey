# Combinatorics Fundamentals

Combinatorics is the study of counting, arranging, and combining objects.

## Core Counting Rules

1.  **Rule of Product (Multiplication Principle):**
    If one task can be performed in $P$ ways and a second independent task can be performed in $Q$ ways, then the two tasks can be performed sequentially in $P \cdot Q$ ways.
2.  **Rule of Sum (Addition Principle):**
    If a task can be performed either in $P$ ways or in $Q$ ways (and the two options are mutually exclusive), the task can be performed in $P + Q$ ways.

## Permutations vs. Combinations

*   **Permutations:** Order matters. Arranging objects.
*   **Combinations:** Order does not matter. Selecting groups.
